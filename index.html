ShortsEngine Uploader

A private, single-owner tool for publishing videos to its owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

ShortsEngine Uploader runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads short educational animations the owner made to the owner's own YouTube channel. It sets each video's title, description and privacy (private or unlisted), and reads back basic view statistics for those same videos.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and it never accesses anyone else's Google account or channel.

Google data it uses

  • YouTube upload: to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube read-only: to confirm which channel it is connected to and check an upload's privacy status.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only: to read view and retention numbers for the owner's own videos.

Details are in the privacy policy.
