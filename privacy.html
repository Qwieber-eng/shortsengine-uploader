Privacy policy

ShortsEngine Uploader · Effective October 28, 2026

Summary

ShortsEngine Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account. It does not collect, sell or share personal information about anyone.

Information it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only for the owner's own channels, it accesses: the channel name (to confirm the right channel is connected), the videos it uploads with their titles, descriptions and privacy status, and view and retention statistics for those videos.

How the information is used

Only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Storage

The Google sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Data from Google accounts is not sent to any other server, and nothing is shared with or sold to third parties.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google account's third-party connections page. Deleting the stored token on the owner's computer also ends access.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
